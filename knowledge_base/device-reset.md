# Resetting your device

A device reset (also called a reboot or power cycle) restarts your router or phone
without erasing any of your settings or data. It is a safe, reversible action and
is free.

A reset usually takes about 60 seconds to complete. After the device powers back
on, give it up to two minutes to reconnect to the network before testing again.

Resetting clears temporary network glitches such as a dropped connection, slow
speeds, or a device showing as offline. If the problem returns repeatedly after a
reset, the issue is likely upstream and should be escalated to a specialist.
