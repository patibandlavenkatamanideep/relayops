# Why a device keeps going offline

A device that repeatedly drops offline is most often caused by weak signal,
interference from other electronics, or an out-of-date device firmware version.

Before contacting support, move the router away from microwaves and cordless
phones, make sure it is not inside a cabinet, and confirm the firmware is current.
Most firmware updates install automatically overnight.

If the device still drops after a reset and a firmware check, there may be a line
fault or an account-level provisioning problem, which a specialist must resolve.
