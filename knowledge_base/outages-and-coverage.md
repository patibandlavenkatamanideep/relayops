# Network outages and coverage

A local outage can make every device on your account appear offline at once, even
though nothing is wrong with your equipment. You can check for outages in your
area from the status page in the mobile app.

During a confirmed outage there is no need to reset your devices; service returns
automatically once the outage is cleared. Estimated restoration times are posted
on the same status page and updated as work progresses.

Coverage in a new location depends on the nearest tower. If you have recently
moved, run a coverage check for your new address before assuming a device fault.
